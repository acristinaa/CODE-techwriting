# Jointly

Jointly is a mobile expense-tracking app designed for individuals and partners who want a clear, shared picture of where their money goes. Whether you're managing your own budget or splitting costs with a roommate, partner, or friend, Jointly keeps everything organized in one place.

### Features

* **Daily Expense Logging** — Quickly add expenses with a category, amount, and optional note
* **Solo & Shared Modes** — Use Jointly by yourself or invite a partner into a Shared Space to track expenses together
* **Bill Splitting** — Split any expense evenly, by percentage, or by custom amounts
* **Budget Categories & Limits** — Create budgets for categories like groceries, dining out, or rent with spending alerts
* **Monthly Reports & Charts** — Visual breakdowns of spending by category and trends over time
* **Reminders & Notifications** — Get notified about upcoming bills, new shared expenses, or budget limits
