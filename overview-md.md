# Overview.md

## Jointly

### What Is Jointly?

Jointly is a mobile expense-tracking application that lets users manage their personal finances on their own or share expenses with a partner, roommate, or friend. It combines the functionality of a personal budget tracker with collaborative bill-splitting tools, all within a single app.

### The Problem

Managing money is already challenging for one person (me to be specific). When shared expenses enter the picture —such as rent, groceries, utilities, and dinners out things get complicated fast. Most people resort to a patchwork of tools: a budgeting app for personal spending, a separate bill-splitting app for shared costs, and maybe a spreadsheet that nobody keeps up to date.&#x20;

### The Solution

Jointly bridges that gap by giving every user two layers of financial tracking. The first is a **Personal Feed** — a private log of all individual expenses, budgets, and reports that only the user can see. The second is **Shared Spaces** — collaborative environments where two or more users can log joint expenses, split bills, set shared budgets, and track who owes what.

The key design principle is that personal and shared finances coexist without interfering with each other. A user's private spending stays private. Shared costs are visible to everyone in that Shared Space. And reports can show either view — or both — depending on what the user needs to see.

### Core Concepts

**Expenses** are the building blocks of Jointly. Each expense has an amount, a category, a date, and an optional note or receipt photo. Expenses can be personal (visible only to the user) or shared (visible to everyone in a Shared Space).

**Shared Spaces** are collaborative groups where two or more users track common expenses. Each Shared Space has its own feed, balance sheet, and budget settings. A user can belong to multiple Shared Spaces — for example, one with a romantic partner for household bills and another with a roommate for rent and utilities.

**Budgets** are monthly spending limits tied to categories. Users can set personal budgets, shared budgets within a Shared Space, or both. Jointly sends notifications when spending approaches or exceeds a budget limit.

**Reports** provide visual summaries of spending over time. They include category breakdowns, month-over-month trends, and balance summaries for Shared Spaces. Reports help users identify patterns and make informed decisions about their spending.

**Settle Up** is the process of resolving balances in a Shared Space. Jointly calculates the net amount owed between partners and provides a clear summary. Actual payment happens outside the app (via Venmo, cash, bank transfer, etc.), and users mark the balance as settled within Jointly.

### Target Audience

Jointly is designed for young adults and working professionals who share living costs or daily expenses with others. This includes couples managing a household budget, roommates splitting rent and groceries, and friends who travel or dine out together frequently. The app is equally useful for solo users who simply want a clean, straightforward expense tracker without the complexity of full-featured financial planning tools.
