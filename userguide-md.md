# UserGuide.md

## Step-by-Step User Guide

A quick guide to getting started with Jointly, from downloading the app to tracking shared expenses with a partner.

***

### Step 1: Download & Create Your Account

Download Jointly from the App Store or Google Play. Open the app and sign up with your email or Google account. Choose a display name — this is what your partners will see in shared spaces.

### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

After signing up, set your default currency and choose a few spending categories to start with (e.g., Food, Transport, Entertainment, Bills). You can add or edit categories anytime in Settings.

### Step 3: Log Your First Expense

Tap the **+** button on the home screen. Enter the amount, pick a category, and add an optional note (e.g., "Coffee with Sam"). Hit **Save** and the expense appears in your personal feed.

### Step 4: Create a Shared Space

Go to **Shared Spaces** and tap **Create New**. Name it (e.g., "Apartment with Alex") and invite your partner by email or shareable link. Once they accept, you both see the same shared feed.

### Step 5: Log a Shared Expense

Inside a Shared Space, tap **+** to add an expense. Enter the details, then choose how to split it — evenly, by percentage, or custom amounts. Jointly automatically calculates who owes what.

### Step 6: Set a Budget

Navigate to **Budgets** and tap **Add Budget**. Pick a category, set a monthly limit, and choose whether it applies to your personal spending, a Shared Space, or both. You'll get alerts at 80% and 100% of your limit.

### Step 7: Review Your Monthly Report

Head to the **Reports** tab to see charts of your spending by category, month-over-month comparisons, and a summary of shared balances. Use this to spot trends and adjust your budgets.

### Step 8: Settle Up

When it's time to square up, go to your Shared Space and tap **Settle Up**. Jointly shows who owes whom and how much. Mark it as settled once payment is made outside the app.
